Vecto - Image to Vector

## Description* (required — this is the field with the red error)
Vecto turns pictures into vector graphics — drop in a PNG or JPG logo, icon,
illustration or scan, and get a clean SVG you can scale to any size and edit
shape by shape.

Open an image and watch it trace live, side by side with the original. Adjust
the colors, style and detail until it looks right, then export the SVG or copy
it straight into Figma, Illustrator, Inkscape or your cutting software.

What you can do:

• Convert PNG, JPG, BMP or GIF into editable SVG
• Compare original and vector side by side with synced zoom
• Straight edges become true straight lines, circles become exact circles
• No hairline gaps between shapes — clean edges at any size
• Pick the number of colors, or let Vecto decide
• Export SVG or PNG, or copy to the clipboard
• Batch-convert from the command line with the included vecto CLI

Works best with flat-color artwork:

• Logos, icons, badges, stickers, flags and flat illustrations
• Clean line drawings and scans
• The largest, sharpest version of the image you have

Not a good fit:

• Smooth gradients and soft shadows: they are traced as bands of color
• Small text: letters can lose their shape, so retype text in your vector editor
• Small or low-resolution images (thumbnails, icons around 128 px): fine detail is lost, so use the largest version you have
• Photos and painterly art: they come out posterized, not photo-real

Everything runs on your own computer — nothing is uploaded, and no account is
needed. Vecto is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and it's open source;
buying it on the Store directly supports development.
